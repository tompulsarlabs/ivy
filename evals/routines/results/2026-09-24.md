# Routine eval, 2026-09-24

This is the A/B behind v10
([tompulsarlabs/ivy#21](https://github.com/tompulsarlabs/ivy/pull/21)), run
again after Ivy reviewed that PR. The review found two problems with the
first run. Each run's working directory carried its case id and variant
label (`check-blocker-nudge-cap.candidate.r1`), the model sees its working
directory, and several case ids name the behaviour under test. The memory
checks were also too loose to prove the edits they claimed. The harness now
names sandboxes randomly, keeps a diff of every file a memory case edits,
and the memory checks read the edit itself. The 2026-09-23 comparison is
withdrawn; its rows stay in git history.

Every row, answer and diff included, is in the `2026-09-24-*.jsonl` files
next to this one, and `scripts/eval-routines.py compare` reads any two.

## The memory rule

Runs passed out of runs scored, by the playbook each candidate ran:

| Case | baseline `2aa1829` | candidate `d40e6da` | candidate `6b59878` | candidate `79c620b` | candidate `f5eefba` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `failsafe-ongoing-condition` | 0/2 | 1/2 | 1/2 | 2/6 | 1/4 |
| `failsafe-ongoing-collapsed` | 0/2 | – | 3/4 | 2/4 | 3/4 |
| the other 7 failsafe cases | 10/14 | 12/14 | 14/14 | 7/7 | 7/7 |

- **`failsafe-ongoing-condition`** replays 2026-09-23 on
  `memory/repos/ivy.md` as it stood: nine dated restatements of the scanner
  outage, each also narrating its own day, and a `## Changelog` entry for
  each. The rule wants the latest restatement moved forward in place. The
  diffs show why runs miss: the latest restatement also carries 09-22's
  events, and rather than drop them, the run adds a sentence after it and
  logs that. No wording moved this: 1 of 2 at `d40e6da`, 1 of 2 at
  `6b59878`, 2 of 6 at `79c620b`, and 1 of 4 at `f5eefba`.
- **`failsafe-ongoing-collapsed`** replays the same day on the page the
  retro leaves behind, with the outage stated once. There every run moved
  the line forward in place, the baseline's too. What remains is a
  `## Changelog` entry for the higher count and the day's outcome. The
  baseline added one in both runs; the candidate did in 1 of 4 runs at
  `6b59878`, 2 of 4 at `79c620b`, and 1 of 4 at `f5eefba`. `f5eefba` says
  what an entry is for: what a page gained or lost. A confirmation, a
  higher count, and the day's outcome are already in the journal and
  `state.json`.
- At these sample sizes the wordings can't be told apart. The head keeps
  `f5eefba`, which states the one rule the collapsed-page runs still broke.
  In production the first retro after merge collapses the page, so from then
  on the failsafe works on a page like the second case's. No wording changed
  the other seven failsafe cases: 14 of 14 at `6b59878`, and 7 of 7 at both
  `79c620b` and `f5eefba`.

## Cost

At list price the candidate costs 26% more than the baseline ($41.52
against $33.00) and takes 33% more turns (28.4 against 21.4 a run).
`6b59878` costs 32% more. The extra turns are reads of the files the rules
point each routine at (files read a run, baseline to candidate):

- failsafe, 21.9 to 31.4: `dispatch/failed/` for contracts that finished
  but were recorded as failed, the memory pages it updates, `state.json`,
  and `memory/ops.md`;
- check, 9.0 to 17.8: the journals, the memory pages of its candidates, and
  the finished contracts under `dispatch/done/` before it chooses and
  records;
- scout, 23.8 to 28.4: each candidate's memory page, `CONTEXT.md`, and
  today's contracts, counted before queueing;
- retro, 23.8 to 29.5: the week's journals, including every `## Check`,
  and the memory pages it collapses.

## Caveats

- Two reps per case is a smoke test. A one-run difference is noise on its
  own.
- Numbers after the A/B are fitted to the cases that prompted them.
- The eval grades decisions given the evidence, not tool use. The evidence
  file stands in for the GitHub MCP lookups, and commits and notifications
  are graded as the routine reports them. Only the three memory cases are
  graded on edits they really made.
- One model at its default effort. `--model` runs the same cases on
  another.
